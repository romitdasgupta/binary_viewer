What the analysis is, and is not

BinaryStudio decodes the structure of Mach-O, ELF and PE files and explains it. Its output can be incomplete or wrong. Files can be malformed on purpose, and some structures are interpreted differently by different loaders.

Labels such as "hardened", findings, anomalies and scores describe what the tool read in the file. They are not a security assessment, a certification, or proof that a file is safe or malicious. Check anything important with other tools before you rely on it.
